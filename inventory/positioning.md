# Positioning Spines

A **spine** is a consistent story about who you are for a given type of role. Every
tailored resume starts from exactly one spine, chosen by `jd-analyst` based on the JD.

Rules:
- Headlines and summaries may only make claims supported by `career-inventory.md`.
- "Lead accomplishments" are inventory IDs, listed in priority order. The tailor
  starts from these but may swap in others that better match a specific JD.
- Keep 2–3 spines. More than that usually means the story is diluted.
